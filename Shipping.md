# Shipping
Effective Date: 2026-05-13

## Order Processing
Most orders placed with Elevated Sensors will ship within 2 business days, unless otherwise noted. All shipping estimates are in business days and are not guaranteed.

## Shipping Options (U.S. Domestic)
We offer three shipping options for most of the United States:

- **Economy (3-8 business days)**: USPS Ground Advantage or UPS Ground Saver
- **Priority (2-5 business days)**: USPS Priority Mail or UPS Ground
- **Expedited (3 business days)**: UPS 2nd Day Air

## International Shipping
We offer affordable international shipping options to a selection of stable global regions: 

- **International Economy**: Asendia e-PAQ is our most affordable option and incurs the lowest additional import related fess. Asendia manages the international transit, with last-mile delivery and customs clearance handled by your local postal service. Depending on your location, you may see a time estimate of 1-4 weeks or 3-8 weeks at checkout.
- **UPS Worldwide Expedited**: Available in select countries. Estimated to arrive in 3-6 business days.
- **DHL Express Worldwide**: Available in select countries. Estimated to arrive in 2-4 business days.

## Import Duties, Taxes, and VAT
All international shipments are shipped “Delivery Duty Unpaid.” The recipient is responsible for paying any import duties, taxes, and VAT upon the shipment’s arrival and customs clearance. Warranty replacements may also incur customs fees.

## Extended Service Areas
For locations classified as “Extended Service Areas” by the shipping carrier, shipping costs may be higher. If this applies to your order, we will contact you to discuss options and ensure that the additional cost is acceptable or suggest a more economical service.
